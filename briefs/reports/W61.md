# W61 — the field-star depth marks carry the 3-D map's own uncertainty

Branch `bmstp-w61`, worktree `../sesnaimpute-w61`. Fits in 15 minutes: one new
reader method pair (`sky/derived/profile.py`), one new grid function and
constant (`bmstp/grid.py`), five new functions (`bmstp/sample_star.py`), one
call-site change (`bmstp/shapes.py`), one region rebuilt at `bmstp.shapes` for
each of Orion A and Ophiuchus.

## Rule implemented

For a retained star of tile `t`, `sigma_x = sigma_A(d) / (A(d) ln 10)`, `A(d)`
and `sigma_A(d)` the tile's own REPRESENTATIVE sightline's `A_CUM_K`/
`SIGMA_COR_K` (`sky.derived.profile._RegionProfile.column_and_sigma`,
interpolated on `DIST_PC`) at the star's own TRILEGAL distance
(`sample_star.star_distances`/`agb_star_distances`, joined through
`STAR_INDEX` into the field-stars product's `DIST_PC` — a field star carries
no sky position of its own, so the tile's own centre
(`population.anchor_tiles`'s `TILE_L_DEG`/`TILE_B_DEG`) picks the nearest
admitted profile sightline as its stand-in, `_RegionProfile.row_of_lb`).
Floored at one grid cell (`grid._X_CELL_WIDTH` = 1/32 dex); capped at that
same sightline's own cloud-interval span, `log10 u(d_back) − log10
u(d_front)`, read against the sightline's own `A_INF_K` so the analytic
far-field tail is not silently dropped when `d_back` (`sample_cloud
.cloud_interval_pc`) reaches past the map's edge. `N_WIDTH_CLASSES = 8`
geometric classes between floor and cap; each star takes the nearest in log
space (`sample_star.star_width_class`). `grid.bin_star_widths` bins and
smooths each populated class's own histogram at its own width (F_4.5 axis
kept at the ordinary one cell) and sums — up to 8 histograms/smooths per
tile in place of 1, no per-star kernel. AGB reuses STAR's tile width classes
and per-star classes (same stars, duplicated O/C as `sample_agb` already
does). YSO/H2S untouched.

Every star in both check tiles lands 2.6–4.5 grid cells wide, squarely in

map's own fractional column sigma is close to constant with distance on
these sightlines, so the class spread is narrow even though the class
*range* (floor to cap) spans nearly two orders of magnitude.

## Effect: the "teeth" (within-span min/max and cells < 1e-3 of peak)

STAR's own `x`-marginal (`GRID_STAR` summed over `F_4.5`), restricted to its
own populated span (first..last cell above `FLOOR * peak`):

| region / tile | before ratio | before n<1e-3 | after ratio | after n<1e-3 | span |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orion A, tile 3 | 1.134e-05 | 86/128 | 1.170e-05 | 48/128 | 128 |
| Ophiuchus, tile 8 | 1.360e-05 | 121/128 | 1.360e-05 | 107/128 | 128 |

The span-edge min/max ratio is essentially unchanged (both ends still taper
to the floor by construction); the interior "teeth" — cells sitting more
than 3 decades below neighbouring peaks — drop by roughly half at Orion A
tile 3 (86→48) and by an eighth at Ophiuchus's tile (121→107), consistent
with a real but tile-dependent smoothing gain (Ophiuchus's own classes are
narrower relative to its span than Orion A's).

## Acceptance identity (rule 11)

Every width forced to the floor (`sigma_classes_dex` = 8 copies of
`grid._X_CELL_WIDTH`, `n_jobs=1` so the monkeypatch reaches the worker code
in-process — loky's process pool otherwise re-imports `sample_star` fresh)
reproduces the pre-run copies **bit-for-bit**: `max|GRID_STAR_new −
GRID_STAR_old| = 0.0`, `max|GRID_AGB_new − GRID_AGB_old| = 0.0`, both
regions. Done once in-process, reported, then the real (non-forced) build
was rerun to regenerate the honest W61 products before the timed
measurements below.

`build_star_family`'s own STAR acceptance line (`x_marginal_max_dev`, sec.
9) reads 0.2843 (Orion A) / 0.3326 (Ophiuchus) rather than ~0: that check
assumes the depth placement is unchanged and only the brightness axis moved
(true of earlier bmstp work); W61 intentionally widens the depth-axis
smoothing per star, so a nonzero deviation there is the expected, correct
signature of this change, not a regression.
