# W59 — the contamination curve attributes an 8 µm excess to nebular light only where nebular light can produce one

Branch `population-w59`, worktree `../sesnaimpute-w59`. Fits in 15 minutes: one
module (`population/pahc_curve.py`), one survey-wide build, one one-region
report-only build.

## Rule implemented

`Q_MIN = EXCESS_SIGMA * s`, `s = 10**(0.4*RESIDUAL_WIDTH_MAG) - 1`
(`q_min_bright_excess`, no new constant: `EXCESS_SIGMA` and
`RESIDUAL_WIDTH_MAG` are the module's own). Every `P_Q` bin whose upper edge
in `q` lies below `Q_MIN` is set to zero (`apply_bright_end_rule`); its
measured, unfloored excess fraction moves to a new `P_Q_BRIGHT_EXCESS`
dataset with its own `N_PER_BIN_BRIGHT_EXCESS`. `Q_MIN` and
`RESIDUAL_WIDTH_MAG` are recorded as product attributes.

## The two comparisons

**AGB dusty share.** The pooled `F_dusty` (§5.2, `f_C F_dusty,C + (1-f_C)
F_dusty,O` = 0.18×0.68 + 0.82×0.24 = **0.32**) bounds every one of the 14
zeroed bins' own `P_Q_BRIGHT_EXCESS` from above (max 0.284, N-weighted mean
0.032): the bright-end excess rate among ALL bright field stars never
exceeds the dusty fraction measured among evolved giants alone, exactly what
is expected since only a minority of field stars are evolved giants at all —
consistent with, not in tension with, attributing the excess to circumstellar
dust rather than an artifact.

**YSO transition-disc share.** The census's own measured `TD` share
(`briefs/reports/W31.md`, survey-wide `yso_population_weight`, 0.4787) is
larger still than every zeroed bin's fraction: the disc-bearing share of the
YSO population itself (a population selected FOR discs) is higher than the
disc/dust share found among unselected bright field stars, as it must be —
the bright-end excess is a small, physically bounded minority consistent
with both class shares, not an unconstrained number.
